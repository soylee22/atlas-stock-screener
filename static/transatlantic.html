Data & methodology

Transatlantic Quality Dividend 50

The universe contains 50 equal-sector-representation quality dividend compounders: the top 25 United States and top 25 European dividend-paying enterprises ranked by cash generation, Return on Equity, dividend track record, and low volatility.

Williams %R and Net Income

Weekly Williams %R measures 14 completed weekly trading sessions (oversold at or below -80, overbought at or above -20). Monthly Williams %R measures 14 completed monthly sessions. Net income reflects trailing twelve months reported net profit converted to USD.
