How the Optimal Dividend Growth Gates & Score Work

Why the Old Atlas Screen Needed Upgrading: The original Atlas preset (ROE ≥ 10%, Div Years ≥ 2, Williams %R ≤ -80, sorted by dollar Net Income) had no dividend growth rate floor, no upper yield-trap cap, and used raw dollar profit as its primary ranking axis. In point-in-time testing across 53,124 stock-weeks (2011–2026), buying oversold stocks with sub-3% dividend growth produced a 43.1% two-year dividend cut rate, whereas requiring ≥7% dividend CAGR reduced the cut rate to 5.9% and lifted 3-year median returns from 9.72% to 11.99% per year.

The 100-Point Optimal Score Formula:
• Pillar 1: Dividend Growth & Streak (0–35 pts): Up to 23 pts for 5Y Dividend CAGR (scaling linearly up to 22%+ CAGR) + up to 12 pts for consecutive dividend growth streak (scaling up to 25+ years).
• Pillar 2: Capital Quality (0–25 pts): Up to 17 pts for Capital Return (best of ROE or 1.4 × ROIC proxy, capped at 35%) + up to 8 pts for Stockopedia Quality Rank (with ROE adjustment for financials where industrial ROCE is not applicable).
• Pillar 3: Pullback Entry Timing (0–25 pts): Up to 20 pts for 14-week Williams %R depth (scaling from -40 down to -100) + up to 5 pts for drawdown below 52-week high (up to 35% off high).
• Pillar 4: Safe Yield Sweet-Spot (0–15 pts): Full 15 pts for yields in the 1.8% to 3.8% sweet spot; scales smoothly for lower-yield right-tail compounders (0.8%–1.8%) and tapers down above 3.8% to penalise >5.5% yield traps.

No Arbitrary Sector Caps: Financials, industrials, technology, healthcare, and consumer compounders compete on the exact same meritocratic scale.
