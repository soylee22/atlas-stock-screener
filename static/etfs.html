DATA COVERAGE

Data & methodology

Coverage and refresh

Listings come from Yahoo’s regional equity screener. A listing market can differ from a company’s home country. Multiple share classes and depositary receipts remain separate rows. Stocks are shown by default. Yahoo also returns some ETFs, warrants and other instruments. The default view excludes recognised non-stock instruments. Germany, Spain, Italy, the Netherlands, Denmark and Sweden use Yahoo equity queries filtered by positive market cap. This reduces secondary-product feeds. Yahoo can still omit market cap from a returned quote. These six imported universes are not full exchange inventories.

Main listings only

Only listings confirmed as primary by TradingView’s stock screener are eligible. Foreign quotations remain excluded even when their primary market is outside our twelve markets. Unknown mappings are excluded until confirmed. OTC quotes, depositary wrappers, preferred securities, funds, warrants and other recognised products remain excluded. Ordinary share classes on the same confirmed primary venue and eligible operating REIT or partnership units remain visible. Country of domicile does not determine the primary exchange. Listing designations are cached and refreshed nightly. A failed metadata refresh retains the previous complete catalogue. Prices, financial statements and dividends remain Yahoo data. Switch this off to see secondary and unconfirmed listings. This is a provider designation, not an exchange-certified primary-listing register.

Quotes and currency rates refresh once a day while the local service runs. Use Refresh data for a new universe scan. Financials load in a rolling queue, prioritising visible rows, and are cached for seven days. Blank metrics mean unavailable or awaiting retrieval.

Company icons

Icons use the company website reported by Yahoo and are fetched through Google’s favicon service. Files are stored locally and reused across listings and share classes. These are website icons and may differ from the logos used by TradingView.

USD conversion

Prices use their quote currency. UK pence prices are converted to pounds first. Market cap uses the base quote currency. Financial statements use their reporting currency. All monetary values use the latest cached Yahoo FX rate, including historical financial totals. Percentages and share volumes retain their original units.

Financial periods

Income and cash flow use four consecutive reported quarters where available, with the latest fiscal year as a fallback. Net margin uses income and revenue for the same period. Capex is a positive expenditure. Free cash flow equals operating cash flow less capex. FCF growth compares the latest two fiscal years and is blank when the previous FCF is non-positive.

Dividend growth

The growth streak counts consecutive increases in total annual dividends per share, ending in the latest completed calendar year. The 5Y rate is the annualised change between six completed calendar-year totals. Calculations use local-currency dividends, so FX movements do not create dividend growth. Yahoo history can be incomplete and can include special dividends. The streak is an observed history, rather than a certified company record.

PRICE AND TREND

Moving averages

Use SMA distance columns or chart axes. Positive means above, negative means below. Filter greater than 0 for above or less than 0 for below.

Standard 200-day and 200-week SMAs stay fixed. This setting changes Custom SMA everywhere: columns, filters, quadrant and exports. Values use Yahoo split-adjusted closes, without dividend adjustment. Current sessions and incomplete weeks are excluded. History is cached progressively, so coverage differs between stocks.
